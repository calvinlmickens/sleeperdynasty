Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Dynasty Sleeper Automation is a private, single-user application operated for personal fantasy-football analysis.

Information accessed

The application accesses public fantasy-football league data from Sleeper and uses Google OAuth authorization to access files and folders in the operator's Google Drive that the application creates or is authorized to use.

How information is used

Information is used only to refresh, validate, download, and store dynasty fantasy-football data and related automation outputs.

Sharing and sale of information

The application does not sell personal information and does not share Google user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. Generated files are stored in the operator's designated Google Drive folder. OAuth access can be revoked through the operator's Google Account security settings.

Data retention and deletion

The operator controls retention and may delete generated files from Google Drive or revoke the application's Google access at any time.

Contact

Questions may be submitted through the project repository.
